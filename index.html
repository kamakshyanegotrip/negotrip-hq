Set up your launcher

Everything here is yours to arrange. Create categories for how you work, then add a tile for each tool you open often.

STEP 1Create a category, for example Research or Sales & Marketing.
STEP 2Add a tool with its name and link, and pick its category.
STEP 3Tap ☆ on the tools you use most so they sit in Pinned.

Use it like an app on your phone
  1. Android (Chrome): open this page, tap ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. iPhone (Safari): open this page, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Your categories, tools and pins are saved in this device's browser. Use Backup below to copy them to another device.
Backup & move to another device
  1. Export on the device that has your tools. Save the file, or copy the text.
  2. Open this page on the other device and tap Import. Pick the file or paste the text.
